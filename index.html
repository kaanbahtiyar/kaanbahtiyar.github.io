Research

Precision motion control and mechatronics

Rapid axis motion excites the structural dynamics of machine tools and other motion systems, leaving residual vibration after each move and cross-talk between axes. Both force longer dwell times and limit productivity. I model and identify the machine's dynamics, and develop data-driven feedback and feedforward controllers that suppress these trajectory-induced vibrations through active dampers and the machine's existing feed drives, for faster settling and higher accuracy.

Active damper control

Auto-tuned feedback

The velocity-feedback law tunes itself online from measured vibration, by solving a band-selective constrained optimization at every time step.

Constrained QP In preparation
70% less damper stroke up to 25% faster settling to 2 µm

Learned feedforward

Iterative learning adds a feedforward force that removes the initial overshoot feedback alone cannot, within stroke and force limits.

Iterative learning CIRP Annals
87% lower peak error 12 → 1.6 µm in about 10 iterations

Cross-axis vibration compensation through the feed drives

A trajectory pre-filter adds a small Z command that cancels the tool-tip vibration excited by fast Y-axis moves, refined by machine-in-the-loop learning.

Trajectory pre-filtering ICPE 2024
80% lower vibration 6 → 1.2 µm at the tool tip

Machining process control

Milling disturbs the machine in several ways, such as forced and chatter vibration from the cutting forces, uneven tooth loads from tool runout, and spindle speed ripple from the cutting torque. I model the kinematics and dynamics of the process, and build adaptive controllers that suppress these process-induced disturbances through the spindle and feed drives, for higher accuracy and productivity.

Chatter detection and adaptive spindle speed control

Lightweight algorithms detect chatter in real time. A separate model-free controller tunes the spindle speed from vibration feedback to remove chatter and minimize vibration.

Extremum seeking CIRP JMST Manufacturing Letters
85% lower RMS vibration with spindle speed control, 131 → 20 m/s²

Tool runout compensation through the feed drives

A micro-circular feed motion cancels tool runout so every tooth cuts the same chip, tuned on the machine from force feedback.

Convex optimization ASME JMSE Patent pending
