Summary

I design, tune and implement controllers that make precision machines settle faster and move more accurately: learned feedforward, auto-tuned feedback, active damping and disturbance compensation. I work from dynamic modeling and system identification through to real-time implementation, and I validate every method on real hardware, within the actuator's stroke and force limits. First author of eleven papers, including three journal articles (CIRP Annals, CIRP JMST, ASME JMSE), co-inventor on a pending patent with an industrial partner, and first place in the 2025 ASPE Student Challenge.

Precision motion control and mechatronics

  • Learned feedforward for point-to-point settling. Cut the settling time to ±2 µm after rapid moves by 98% (29.7 to 0.6 ms) and the peak-to-peak error by 90% (35 to 3.5 µm), compared with feedback-only control. An iterative learning controller learns a feedforward force profile for an active inertial damper in about 10 runs, on a sparse B-spline basis, and enforces the actuator's stroke and force limits in every update. First published in CIRP Annals (2024).
  • Auto-tuned active damping. Formulated the tuning of the damper's velocity-feedback law as a constrained quadratic program, solved from vibration measured over repeated moves. It selects the filter passband and gain automatically, uses 70% less actuator stroke than conventional tuning and settles up to 28% faster, and the tuned controller carries over to other moves.
  • Cross-axis vibration compensation. Identified the coupling on a 3-axis vertical machining center, where Y-axis acceleration rocks the column and vibrates the tool tip in Z, and designed a trajectory pre-filter that cancels it through the Z-axis feed drive without changing the servo loops. A frequency-domain fit gives the initial design and machine-in-the-loop iterations refine it: up to 77% lower tool-tip vibration (5.5 to 1.3 µm), verified with a laser encoder. Young Research Award, ICPE 2024.

Machining process control

  • Spindle-synchronous disturbance compensation. Cancelled the effect of tool runout by adding a micro-circular trajectory, synchronized with the spindle, to the X and Y feed drives in the 5 kHz position loop. Its amplitude and phase are tuned online by convex optimization from force or acceleration feedback and converge in 2 to 3 updates: 86% lower cutting-force variation (101 to 14 N). ASME JMSE (2026), patent pending with an industrial partner.
  • Real-time spindle speed optimization. Built a model-free extremum-seeking controller that optimizes the spindle speed during the cut from accelerometer feedback, integrated through the CNC's standard spindle-override input via the PLC. It lowered RMS vibration by 85% (131 to 20 m/s²), and by 57 to 66% compared with a conventional chatter mitigation strategy. CIRP JMST (2025).
  • Real-time chatter detection. Developed two lightweight detectors, based on a spectral power ratio and on recursive principal component analysis, that separate chatter from forced vibration and flag its onset in real time with a single threshold. Manufacturing Letters (2022).
