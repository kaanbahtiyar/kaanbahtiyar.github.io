Projects / Cross-axis vibration compensation

Cross-axis vibration compensation

On a C-frame machining center, a hard acceleration of the table in Y rocks the column, and the rocking shows up as Z vibration at the tool tip even though the Z axis received no command. I cancel it with a small, synchronized Z-axis command through the machine's existing feed drives.

Trajectory pre-filtering ICPE 2024 Young Research Award

Method

A cross-talk dynamics compensator generates a small Z-axis command from the commanded X and Y motion, shaped so that the commanded and the structurally induced Z motion cancel at the tool tip.

The design has two stages. A frequency-domain fit around the excited rocking mode gives an initial compensator, and a machine-in-the-loop iterative learning pass refines it from measured acceleration, so performance does not depend on a perfect model. It acts as a trajectory pre-filter ahead of the existing servo loops, which stay untouched.

Results

Up to 77% lower Z tool-tip vibration 5.5 → 1.3 µm

Measured during Y-axis moves on a 3-axis vertical machining center. The lower coupling also allows more aggressive axis acceleration, which shortens cycle time.

The work received the Young Research Award at ICPE 2024 (JSPE, Sendai, Japan).
